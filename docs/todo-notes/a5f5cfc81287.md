## Deferred: doctor's "open-vocabulary relations" check

The story's `kb doctor` bullet lists, among its checks, "open-vocab relations
(after C5)". C5 (the `relation` form — `pkg/kb/relation.go`'s `RelationRing`
reader over `<ring>/kb/graph.jsonl`) has **not landed** on this branch: there is
no `relation.go`, no `RelationRing`, and no relation store to read. The check is
impossible to implement without C5's reader, and the bullet itself scopes it
"after C5".

What was done instead: `doctor` implements every other check — dangling links,
orphan pages (no inbound, no outbound, not validated), near-duplicate pairs
(reusing `NearDuplicate`), and missing `form`/`description`. The open-vocab
relations check is the one remaining hook; when C5 lands, add a branch to
`Doctor` that reads the `RelationRing` and flags relations whose verb is outside
the core vocabulary (`about`, `supersedes`, `depends-on`, `calls`, `tested-by`,
`decided-in`, `observed`). No interface change to `Doctor` is forced — a new
slice field on `DoctorReport` plus one loop covers it.
